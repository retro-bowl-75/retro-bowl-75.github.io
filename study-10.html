About this study

Basketball Legends – Arcade Hoops Action

Basketball Legends is an exciting 2D basketball game where you compete in fast-paced matches against AI or friends. Featuring famous basketball-inspired characters with unique abilities, the game mixes realistic shots with fun, exaggerated moves like super dunks and power-ups. You can play solo, in tournaments, or in two-player mode for head-to-head action.

The charm of Basketball Legends is in its mix of skill and chaos. One moment you’re making a clean three-pointer, the next you’re blocking a shot with an over-the-top special move. Matches are quick and addictive, perfect for competitive fun.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to move and jump, and specific keys (Z/X or K/L depending on mode) to shoot, pass, or activate special skills. In two-player mode, each player has separate controls. The goal is simple — score more points than your opponent before time runs out!
