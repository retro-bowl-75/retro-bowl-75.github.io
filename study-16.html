Drift Hunters – Master the Art of Drifting

Drift Hunters is a 3D drifting game where style matters just as much as speed. You can choose from a wide selection of customizable cars, upgrade their performance, and take them to various tracks designed for drifting. The goal is to chain drifts together to earn points and unlock better vehicles.

The game’s realistic physics make drifting feel smooth and satisfying. Each car handles differently, so experimenting with setups and techniques is key to mastering the tracks. With no strict time limits, you can practice and perfect your skills at your own pace.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys or WASD to drive, Space for the handbrake, and C to change the camera view. On mobile, use virtual steering and pedal buttons. Keep your drifts long and controlled to rack up big combos, and aim for the highest score possible.
