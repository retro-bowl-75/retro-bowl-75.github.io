Tunnel Rush – Speed and Reflex Challenge

Tunnel Rush is a high-speed arcade game that tests your reflexes as you race through a colorful, twisting tunnel filled with obstacles. The tunnel changes shape and color as you progress, keeping you on edge and forcing you to react quickly. The goal is simple — survive as long as you can without hitting anything.

The game’s vibrant visuals and pulsating music create an adrenaline-filled experience that’s both challenging and addictive. Each run feels fresh thanks to the constantly changing patterns and speeds.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to move left or right. On mobile, tilt your device or swipe the screen to dodge obstacles. Stay focused, anticipate turns, and make split-second decisions to keep going. The longer you survive, the higher your score will be. Can you master the tunnel?
