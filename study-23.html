Eggy Car – Drive Carefully or Crack the Egg

Eggy Car is a fun and challenging driving game where your mission is to transport a fragile egg across bumpy roads without breaking it. You must drive slowly and balance your vehicle to keep the egg from falling, all while climbing hills and crossing tricky terrain.

The charm of Eggy Car lies in its simplicity mixed with tension — one wrong move, and your egg is gone. As you progress, you’ll collect coins to unlock new vehicles with different handling.

How to Play
On PC, use the right arrow key to accelerate and the left arrow key to reverse, adjusting your speed to keep the egg stable. On mobile, tap the on-screen pedals to control your car. The key to winning is patience — smooth driving will get you farther than rushing. How far can you go without cracking the egg?
