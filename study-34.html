Drift Boss – One-Button Drifting Fun

Drift Boss is a simple yet addictive drifting game where you guide a car along a narrow, winding track without falling off. The road is full of sharp turns, and your only job is to time your drifts perfectly to stay on course. As you progress, the track becomes trickier with tighter corners and unexpected angles.

The game’s appeal lies in its easy controls and challenging gameplay. It’s perfect for quick sessions but hard to master, especially when aiming for high scores and collecting rewards to unlock new vehicles.

How to Play
On PC, press the space bar or click the mouse to make your car drift right, and release to go straight. On mobile, tap and hold to drift, then release to straighten. Anticipate turns, control your timing, and see how far you can go without falling off the track.
