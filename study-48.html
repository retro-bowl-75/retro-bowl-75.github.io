Tank Trouble – Chaotic Tank Battles

Tank Trouble is a fast-paced arcade battle game where you control a small tank in a maze and try to blast your opponents before they get you. The bullets bounce off walls, which makes aiming both tricky and strategic — you can hit enemies directly or set up ricochet shots for surprise attacks.

You can play solo against AI, or challenge friends in 2 or 3-player local matches. The random maze layouts keep every round fresh, and the controls are simple enough for quick, chaotic fun.

How to Play
On PC, Player 1 uses the arrow keys to move and “M” to shoot. Player 2 uses “E, S, D, F” to move and “Q” to shoot, while Player 3 uses the mouse. On mobile, use touch controls to steer and fire. Predict enemy moves, use walls to your advantage, and become the last tank standing!
