About this study

Five Nights at Freddy’s – Survive the Night

Five Nights at Freddy’s is a suspenseful horror game where you work as a night guard at Freddy Fazbear’s Pizza. Your job is to monitor security cameras and survive five nights while avoiding haunted animatronic characters that roam the building after hours.

The game builds tension through limited resources — you have a finite amount of power to control doors, lights, and cameras. Every sound, flicker, or shadow could mean danger, and one mistake can lead to a terrifying jumpscare.

How to Play
On PC, use the mouse to check cameras, close doors, and turn on lights. On mobile, tap the screen to interact with controls. Conserve power, watch for movement, and react quickly when animatronics get close. The key to winning is staying calm under pressure and managing your resources wisely. Can you make it through all five nights?
