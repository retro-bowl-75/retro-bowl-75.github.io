Vex 3 – Precision Platforming Challenge

Vex 3 is a fast-paced platform game where you guide a stickman through dangerous obstacle courses filled with spikes, saw blades, and tricky jumps. Each level, called an “Act,” tests your reflexes and timing, requiring you to run, jump, slide, and swim your way to the finish.

The game’s clean visuals, smooth controls, and challenging level design make it both addictive and rewarding. Hidden checkpoints help you progress, but one mistake sends you back to the last save point — so precision is key.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys or WASD to move, jump, and crouch. On mobile, use on-screen arrows and buttons. Time your moves carefully, avoid hazards, and look for shortcuts to complete each stage faster. With patience and skill, you can conquer every act and prove yourself a true Vex master.
