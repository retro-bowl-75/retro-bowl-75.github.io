Wheely 8: Aliens – A Puzzle Adventure with a Twist

Wheely 8: Aliens is a charming point-and-click puzzle game where the lovable red car Wheely goes on an intergalactic adventure. After encountering a pair of friendly aliens who need help repairing their spaceship, Wheely sets out on a journey full of clever puzzles, obstacles, and surprises.

Each level is a unique challenge that requires you to interact with objects, activate mechanisms, and solve mini-puzzles to help Wheely progress. The mix of cute animations and brain-teasing gameplay makes it enjoyable for players of all ages.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to click on objects, buttons, and Wheely himself to make him move. On mobile, tap the screen to interact. Timing and observation are key — experiment with different actions to discover the solution. Help Wheely assist his alien friends and complete this adorable space-themed adventure!
