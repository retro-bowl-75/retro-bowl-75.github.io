Run 3 – Endless Space Running Adventure

Run 3 is an addictive endless runner set in outer space, where you guide a small alien through a series of tunnels and platforms. The twist? You can run on walls and rotate the tunnel to avoid falling into the void. Each level gets more challenging with gaps, moving platforms, and tricky angles to master.

The game offers an endless mode for high scores and an adventure mode with progressively harder levels. Its simple controls and unique gravity mechanic make it both easy to pick up and hard to put down.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to move left or right and jump. On mobile, swipe to steer and tap to jump. Time your movements carefully, switch walls when necessary, and keep running as far as you can without falling into space!
