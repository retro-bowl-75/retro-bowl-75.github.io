Sniper Shot – Precision Shooting Challenge

Sniper Shot is an intense shooting game that tests your accuracy, patience, and timing. As a skilled sniper, your mission is to eliminate targets from long distances using a powerful rifle. Each level presents different objectives, from taking down moving enemies to hitting high-value targets with limited ammunition.

The challenge comes from reading the environment — you’ll need to account for movement, timing, and sometimes even obstacles that block your view. Precision is more important than speed, so every shot should count.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to aim and click to fire. You can zoom in with the right mouse button or a dedicated zoom key, depending on the game version. On mobile, drag to aim and tap to shoot. Study your target, take a deep breath, and pull the trigger at the perfect moment to secure the kill.
