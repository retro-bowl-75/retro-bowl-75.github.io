Spider Solitaire – Classic Card Game Challenge

Spider Solitaire is a strategic single-player card game that tests your patience and planning skills. The goal is to arrange all the cards in descending order from King to Ace in the same suit. Once a full sequence is completed, it’s removed from the board. The game continues until all cards are cleared or no more moves are possible.

You can choose different difficulty levels — from playing with one suit for beginners to all four suits for a real challenge. Each level requires careful thinking to avoid getting stuck.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to drag and drop cards. On mobile, tap and drag to move them. Build sequences in descending order, and when possible, move cards of the same suit together for easier completion. Use the stockpile for new cards, but plan ahead — every move counts in Spider Solitaire!
