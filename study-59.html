About this study

Penalty Shooters 3 – Ultimate Penalty Kick Challenge

Penalty Shooters 3 is a competitive football game focused entirely on the drama of penalty shootouts. You choose your team, step up to the spot, and try to outsmart the goalkeeper while also defending your own net. Timing, accuracy, and prediction are the keys to victory.

The game features multiple leagues and tournaments, letting you progress from smaller competitions to championship finals. Every kick matters — one mistake could cost you the match.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to aim and click to shoot or dive when playing as goalkeeper. On mobile, tap and swipe in the direction you want to shoot or save. When attacking, aim carefully and adjust your shot power. When defending, read your opponent’s movement and react quickly. Win the shootout by scoring more goals than your rival and lead your team to glory!
