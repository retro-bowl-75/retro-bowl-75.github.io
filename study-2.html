About this study

YoHoHo.io – Pirate Battle Royale Fun

YoHoHo.io is a fast-paced pirate-themed battle royale game where your goal is simple: be the last pirate standing. You spawn on a tropical island filled with treasure chests, gold coins, and rival pirates. As you collect coins, your pirate grows in size and strength, making it easier to overpower smaller opponents. The more loot you gather, the more dominant you become — but beware, bigger pirates are also slower, so agility can be just as important as raw power.

The game is easy to pick up but challenging to master. Matches are quick, intense, and packed with action, making it perfect for both short breaks and longer play sessions. Between battles, you can spend your collected loot to unlock new pirate characters, each with their own unique style.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse or arrow keys/WASD to move your pirate around the island. Click or press the Space bar to attack. Hold your attack to charge a powerful dash, which can quickly close the distance to an enemy or deliver a decisive finishing blow. Timing your dash is crucial — use it too early, and you might miss your target; too late, and they could strike first.
