About this study

Basketball Stars – Fast-Paced Hoops Action

Basketball Stars is an exciting 1v1 basketball game where you face off against opponents in quick, skill-based matches. You can dribble, shoot, and block in real-time, using timing and strategy to outscore your rival. The game offers different modes, from shooting races to full matches, keeping gameplay fresh and competitive.

With smooth animations and responsive controls, every play feels dynamic and satisfying. You can also customize your player’s look and unlock new basketballs as you progress.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to move and jump, and specific keys (Z/X) to steal or shoot. On mobile, swipe and tap to control movement and actions. Fake out your opponent, time your shots perfectly, and aim for three-pointers or dunks. The key to victory is quick reflexes and smart plays!
