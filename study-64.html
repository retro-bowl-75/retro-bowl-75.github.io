G-Switch – Gravity-Defying Runner

G-Switch is a fast-paced running game where you control a character who can flip gravity with a single command. Your goal is to run as far as possible, switching between the floor and ceiling to avoid obstacles and gaps. The challenge comes from the increasing speed and the need for perfect timing to survive.

The game can be played solo, in challenge mode, or in exciting multiplayer matches where up to 8 players compete on the same screen. Quick reflexes and precise gravity flips are the keys to victory.

How to Play
On PC, press the “X” key (or the assigned key for your player) to flip gravity. In multiplayer, each player uses their own control key. On mobile, tap the screen to switch gravity. Anticipate upcoming obstacles, flip at the right time, and try to outlast your opponents to become the ultimate gravity runner!
