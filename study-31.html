About this study

Paper.io – Conquer the Arena

Paper.io is a competitive territory-capturing game where your goal is to control as much of the map as possible. You move around the arena, leaving a trail behind you, and any enclosed space becomes your territory. The challenge is to expand without letting opponents cut through your trail — if they do, you’re out!

The game is fast, simple, and highly addictive. You can play defensively by protecting your base or go on the offensive, stealing territory from others. Every match is a mix of risk-taking and smart strategy.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to change direction. On mobile, swipe your finger in the desired direction. Stay within your territory for safety, but venture out to expand your area. Watch your trail closely and take down opponents by crossing theirs. The bigger your territory, the closer you are to dominating the map!
