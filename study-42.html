Wheely 6: Fairytale – A Magical Puzzle Journey

Wheely 6: Fairytale is a whimsical puzzle-adventure game where our curious red car finds himself in a magical fairytale world. Transported into a land of castles, dragons, and knights, Wheely must solve clever puzzles to complete his quest and return home.

Each level is packed with interactive elements like levers, gates, and hidden objects. The fairytale theme adds a charming twist, with fantasy-inspired obstacles and lighthearted animations that make the adventure even more engaging.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to click on Wheely, buttons, and objects to trigger events. On mobile, tap the screen to interact. Timing and observation are key — some puzzles require activating the right items in the correct order. Guide Wheely through this enchanting world, outsmart tricky challenges, and live happily ever after in this magical car adventure!
