Papa’s Pizzeria – Bake, Slice, and Serve

Papa’s Pizzeria is a fun time-management game where you take over Papa Louie’s pizza shop. Your job is to take orders, prepare pizzas with the correct toppings, bake them perfectly, and slice them just right before serving. Customer satisfaction is key — the better your performance, the more tips you earn.

Each day brings new challenges, from more complex orders to more customers arriving at once. You can use your earnings to upgrade the shop and improve your efficiency.

How to Play
On PC, use the mouse to navigate between stations: Order Station to take orders, Topping Station to add ingredients, Baking Station to cook the pizza, and Cutting Station to slice it. On mobile, tap and drag to interact. Accuracy and timing matter — keep customers happy to build your reputation and become the best pizza chef in town!
