Snake.io – Classic Snake with a Modern Twist

Snake.io is a fast-paced multiplayer game inspired by the classic snake concept but with an online competitive twist. You control a colorful snake, eating glowing orbs to grow longer while avoiding collisions with other snakes. The more you eat, the bigger you become — and the more dangerous you are to opponents.

The excitement comes from outsmarting other players by trapping them or forcing them to crash into you. Matches are quick, addictive, and perfect for casual fun or competitive play.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys or mouse to steer your snake, and hold the space bar to boost speed. On mobile, drag your finger to move and tap for a quick boost. Collect orbs, avoid bigger snakes, and use clever maneuvers to climb the leaderboard. Survive as long as possible to claim the top spot!
