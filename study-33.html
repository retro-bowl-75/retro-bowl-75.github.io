Cookie Clicker – The Sweetest Idle Game

Cookie Clicker is an addictive idle clicker game where your goal is to bake as many cookies as possible. It starts simply — click the giant cookie to produce more — but soon, you’ll be buying grandmas, farms, factories, and even magical portals to increase your cookie production automatically.

The fun comes from watching your cookie count skyrocket and unlocking quirky upgrades and achievements. The more you play, the faster your production grows, turning small clicks into massive cookie empires.

How to Play
On PC, click the giant cookie to start baking. Use your cookies to purchase upgrades and buildings that produce cookies automatically. On mobile, tap the cookie and navigate menus to buy improvements. Keep upgrading to boost production and aim for rare achievements. In Cookie Clicker, the only limit is how many cookies you can dream of baking!
