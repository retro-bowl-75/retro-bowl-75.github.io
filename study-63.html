Stick Merge – Merge, Upgrade, and Defend

Stick Merge is an addictive idle-action game where your goal is to combine identical weapons to create stronger ones. You control a stickman positioned behind cover, automatically shooting enemies that approach. The stronger your weapons, the faster you can take down waves of attackers and earn coins.

The game mixes strategy and action — you must manage your weapon slots, merge wisely to unlock powerful gear, and decide when to upgrade your damage or firing speed. The further you progress, the tougher your enemies become.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to drag and drop identical weapons together to merge them. On mobile, tap and hold to move weapons into position. Keep merging to unlock better firepower, and use your coins to boost your shooting stats. Defend your position as long as possible and aim to become the ultimate stickman sharpshooter!
