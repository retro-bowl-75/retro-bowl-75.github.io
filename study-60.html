About this study

Truck Simulator – Realistic Driving Experience

Truck Simulator is a detailed driving game that lets you step into the role of a professional truck driver. Your mission is to transport cargo across different routes, following traffic rules and managing your time. The game features realistic truck handling, road environments, and weather conditions, making every delivery a true test of skill and patience.

You can choose from various trucks, upgrade them, and explore both city roads and long highways. Successful deliveries earn money, which can be used for vehicle improvements.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys or WASD to steer, accelerate, and brake. Use the mouse or specific keys for indicators, headlights, and other controls. On mobile, use the on-screen pedals and steering buttons. Follow road signs, avoid collisions, and complete your deliveries on time to become a top truck driver.
