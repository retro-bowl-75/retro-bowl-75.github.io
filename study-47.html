Traffic Control – Manage the Flow

Traffic Control is a fast-paced management game where you oversee busy intersections and guide vehicles safely to their destinations. Your job is to prevent crashes by controlling traffic lights and keeping the flow moving smoothly. The challenge increases as more cars, buses, and trucks enter the road, requiring quick reflexes and sharp observation.

The game tests your multitasking skills — one moment you’re letting cars pass, the next you’re stopping traffic to avoid accidents. Timing and awareness are key to keeping the roads safe.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to click on traffic lights or vehicles to control their movement. On mobile, tap the screen to interact. Change lights at the right moment to prevent congestion and collisions. The longer you keep traffic moving without accidents, the higher your score. How long can you keep the city running smoothly?
