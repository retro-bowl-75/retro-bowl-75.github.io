Drive Mad – Crazy Off-Road Challenge

Drive Mad is a fun and unpredictable driving game where your goal is to reach the finish line without flipping over. Each level presents a unique obstacle course filled with ramps, gaps, moving platforms, and tricky terrain. The challenge comes from balancing speed with control — go too slow and you won’t make jumps, but go too fast and you’ll crash.

The game’s quirky physics and creative level design make every stage feel fresh. As you progress, the courses get harder, testing your timing, patience, and driving skills.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys or WASD to accelerate, brake, and tilt your vehicle. On mobile, use the on-screen pedals for control. Adjust your speed carefully and use small tilts to keep your truck stable. The key is finding the perfect balance to complete each crazy track.
