Wheelie Bike 2 – Balance and Skill Challenge

Wheelie Bike 2 is a fun and addictive balancing game where your goal is to ride as far as possible while keeping your bike on one wheel. The challenge comes from maintaining perfect balance over uneven roads, ramps, and obstacles without letting your front wheel touch the ground.

The simple one-button control makes it easy to start, but mastering the right timing and precision takes practice. As you progress, the terrain becomes trickier, testing your reflexes and patience.

How to Play
On PC, press and hold the space bar or click the mouse to lift your bike’s front wheel, and release to bring it down. On mobile, tap and hold the screen to wheelie, then release when needed. The key is to adjust your balance according to the slope — too much tilt and you’ll fall backward, too little and your front wheel will drop.
