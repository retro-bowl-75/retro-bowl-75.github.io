Time Shooter SWAT – Time Moves When You Move

Time Shooter SWAT is a unique first-person shooter that blends tactical planning with slow-motion action. The core mechanic is simple yet brilliant: time only moves when you move. This means you can pause mid-action, observe enemy positions, and carefully plan your next step. Instead of relying purely on reflexes, you’ll need sharp thinking and precision.

You play as an elite SWAT operative sent into dangerous missions where heavily armed enemies are waiting around every corner. Each level is like a puzzle — enemies have different weapons, cover positions vary, and every movement you make changes the battlefield. You’ll need to grab weapons, dodge bullets, and eliminate threats while keeping yourself alive.

How to Play
On PC, use the WASD keys to move and the mouse to aim. Left-click to shoot, right-click to throw objects or switch weapons. Remember: if you stop moving, time slows down dramatically, allowing you to dodge incoming fire or line up the perfect shot. Always scan your surroundings before committing to your next action.
