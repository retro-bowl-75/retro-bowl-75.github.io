Snail Bob 5 – Love Story Adventure

Snail Bob 5: Love Story is a charming puzzle-platform game following Bob the snail on a romantic quest. After seeing a beautiful singer on TV, Bob sets out on a journey to find her. Along the way, he must overcome tricky traps, moving platforms, and clever puzzles.

Each level requires you to safely guide Bob to the exit. You’ll need to activate levers, press buttons, and interact with various mechanisms in the right order to clear obstacles. Careful timing and observation are the keys to success.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to stop or start Bob, and click objects in the scene to trigger actions. On mobile, tap the screen to interact and control Bob’s movement. Avoid hazards, collect hidden stars for bonus points, and help Bob complete his love-filled journey!
