Flappy Bird – Simple Yet Addictive

Flappy Bird is a minimalist yet incredibly addictive arcade game that tests your timing and patience. You control a small bird trying to fly through endless rows of green pipes without crashing. The concept is simple, but the challenge is real — one wrong tap and it’s game over.

The game’s retro pixel art style and upbeat soundtrack make it charming, but what truly hooks players is the "just one more try" feeling. Every run pushes you to beat your previous high score, and the difficulty keeps you coming back for more.

How to Play
On PC, press the Space bar or click the mouse to make the bird flap upward. On mobile, tap the screen to keep your bird in the air. The key is to find a rhythm and stay calm — rushing usually leads to quick failure. Pass through as many pipes as possible to climb the leaderboard!
