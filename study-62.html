Zombie Shooter 2D – Survival Action Challenge

Zombie Shooter 2D is a side-scrolling action game where you must survive waves of relentless zombies. Armed with a variety of weapons, your goal is to eliminate all enemies on each level before they overwhelm you. The simple 2D design makes the gameplay easy to follow, but the increasing number and speed of zombies will test your reflexes and accuracy.

Each stage offers different layouts and obstacles, requiring you to move strategically and manage your ammunition. Power-ups and new weapons help you stay alive longer.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys or WASD to move and the mouse to aim and shoot. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick for movement and tap buttons to fire or switch weapons. Keep moving to avoid being trapped, aim for headshots to deal more damage, and clear all zombies to advance to the next level.
