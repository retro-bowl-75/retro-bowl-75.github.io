Papa’s Bakeria – Bake, Serve, and Succeed

Papa’s Bakeria is a fun time-management and cooking game where you run a busy bakery inside a shopping mall. Customers come in with specific pie orders, and it’s your job to bake them perfectly. You’ll choose the crust, add fillings, bake to the right time, and decorate with toppings before serving.

The challenge comes from juggling multiple orders at once while keeping customers happy. The better your performance, the higher your tips, which you can use to buy upgrades and decorations for your shop.

How to Play
On PC, use the mouse to navigate between stations — order, build, bake, and top. Follow each customer’s request carefully and manage your time to handle several pies at once. On mobile, tap and drag to prepare ingredients and interact with the bakery tools. Accuracy and speed are key to becoming the best baker in town!
