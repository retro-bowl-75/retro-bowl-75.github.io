OvO – Precision Platforming Challenge

OvO is a fast-paced platform game that focuses on speed, precision, and timing. You control a small stickman-like character who can run, jump, slide, and wall-jump through obstacle-filled levels. Each stage is designed to test your reflexes with spikes, moving platforms, and tricky gaps to clear.

The game’s minimalist graphics and smooth controls make it easy to pick up but hard to master. The real challenge comes from completing levels as quickly as possible, perfect for speedrunning fans.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to move, jump, and slide. You can jump higher by combining momentum with wall jumps or slide jumps. On mobile, use the on-screen arrows and jump button to navigate. Learn each level’s layout, time your moves perfectly, and try to beat your best completion time!
