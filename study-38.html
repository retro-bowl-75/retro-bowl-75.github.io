About this study

2048 – Add, Merge, and Win

2048 is a simple yet highly addictive puzzle game where your goal is to combine numbered tiles to reach the 2048 tile. The game is played on a 4x4 grid, and each move slides all tiles in one direction. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one, doubling their value.

The challenge comes from planning your moves to avoid filling up the board too quickly. One wrong move can trap you, ending the game before you reach your target.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to slide tiles. On mobile, swipe in the direction you want to move. Focus on keeping your largest tile in a corner and build around it. Combine tiles strategically to create higher numbers, and aim to reach the legendary 2048 tile — or push even further for higher scores!
