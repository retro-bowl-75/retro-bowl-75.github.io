Monkey Mart – Fun Supermarket Adventure

Monkey Mart is a cute and casual management game where you run a supermarket as an adorable monkey. Your job is to plant crops, harvest them, and use the ingredients to stock your store. Customers will arrive looking for fresh produce, snacks, and other goods, and it’s up to you to keep the shelves full.

The game’s charm comes from its simple, relaxing gameplay and colorful, cartoon-style graphics. As you earn money, you can expand your market, unlock new products, and even hire helpers to keep things running smoothly.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys or WASD to move your monkey around. Collect items, place them on shelves, and serve customers to earn coins. On mobile, use the touch joystick to move. Keep upgrading your store to attract more customers and grow your supermarket empire!
