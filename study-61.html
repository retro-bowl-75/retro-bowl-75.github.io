World’s Hardest Game – Precision and Patience Test

World’s Hardest Game is a challenging puzzle-platform game that pushes your reflexes, timing, and focus to the limit. Your goal is to guide a small red square through mazes filled with moving blue dots, collecting yellow coins before reaching the green safe zone. One touch from a blue enemy sends you back to the start.

The game’s simple design hides its brutal difficulty — every level requires careful planning, split-second timing, and flawless execution. It’s a true test of skill for players who love a challenge.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to move in four directions. On mobile, swipe in the direction you want to go. Plan your path, wait for the perfect moment, and move quickly to avoid enemies. Collect all coins in the level to unlock the goal and advance. Precision and patience are the keys to victory!
