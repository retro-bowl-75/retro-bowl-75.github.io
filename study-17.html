Snow Rider 3D – Downhill Sledding Adventure

Snow Rider 3D is a thrilling winter sports game where you race down snowy slopes on a sled, dodging obstacles and collecting gifts along the way. The endless downhill run is full of ramps, snowmen, and moving hazards, keeping you on your toes as the speed increases. Your goal is to survive as long as possible while racking up the highest score.

The game’s colorful 3D graphics and smooth controls make it both relaxing and exciting. The more you play, the better you get at predicting obstacles and making quick maneuvers.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to steer left and right. On mobile, swipe your finger in the direction you want to move. Avoid crashing into obstacles, hit jumps for extra fun, and collect gifts to boost your score. How far can you ride without wiping out?
