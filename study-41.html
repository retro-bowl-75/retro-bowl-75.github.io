About this study

Wheely 7: Detective – Solve the Mystery

Wheely 7: Detective is a charming puzzle-adventure game where the lovable red car Wheely takes on the role of a detective. After a daring robbery, Wheely sets out to track down the culprit by following clues, solving puzzles, and outsmarting tricky obstacles.

Each level is filled with interactive objects, hidden mechanisms, and clever challenges. You’ll need to pay attention to details, think logically, and use the environment to help Wheely progress in his investigation. The mix of humor, cute animations, and brain-teasing puzzles makes it fun for all ages.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to click on Wheely, levers, buttons, and other objects to trigger actions. On mobile, tap the screen to interact. Timing matters — some puzzles require activating multiple objects in the right order. Help Wheely crack the case and bring the thief to justice!
