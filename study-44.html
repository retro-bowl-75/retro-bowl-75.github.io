About this study

Whack Your Boss – Stress Relief with Dark Humor

Whack Your Boss is a point-and-click game designed for comical stress relief, where you find creative (and exaggerated) ways to “deal” with an annoying boss. Set in a small office, you can click on different objects to trigger over-the-top animations, each showing a unique and absurd outcome.

The game doesn’t take itself seriously — its cartoonish style and outrageous scenarios make it more about humor than violence. It’s meant as a lighthearted, fictional escape from workplace frustration.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to click on objects in the office. Each clickable item triggers a unique animation, so explore carefully to find them all. On mobile, tap the screen to interact. There’s no time limit or score — the goal is simply to discover every hidden scenario and enjoy the game’s dark comedic style.
