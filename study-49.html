Parking Fury 2 – Precision Parking Challenge

Parking Fury 2 is a skill-based driving game where your goal is to park various vehicles in tight spots without crashing. Each level presents a different parking scenario — from simple straight spaces to tricky angles and narrow lanes. The game tests your precision, patience, and control behind the wheel.

The fun comes from mastering each vehicle’s handling while avoiding obstacles like traffic and pedestrians. As levels progress, the challenges become more complex, requiring sharper turns and better timing.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to steer, accelerate, and reverse. On mobile, use the on-screen steering and pedal controls. Follow the arrows to the designated parking spot, align your vehicle carefully, and stop within the marked area. Avoid hitting other cars or barriers, or you’ll have to restart the level. The smoother your parking, the better your score!
