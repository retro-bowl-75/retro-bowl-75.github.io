About this study

Slope – High-Speed Neon Descent

Slope is a fast-paced 3D running game where you control a ball rolling down an endless, neon-colored slope. The track is filled with sharp turns, steep drops, and moving obstacles, all while your speed increases the farther you go. The goal is simple — survive as long as possible and aim for the highest score.

The game’s smooth physics and minimalistic yet vibrant visuals make it both thrilling and addictive. Every run is different, and the challenge comes from reacting quickly to sudden twists in the track.

How to Play
On PC, use the left and right arrow keys or A/D keys to steer the ball. On mobile, tilt your device or swipe to change direction. Avoid red obstacles, stay centered on the path, and anticipate turns to keep your run going. One wrong move, and it’s game over!
