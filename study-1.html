Retro Bowl – Relive the Glory Days of American Football

Retro Bowl is a charming blend of classic pixel art and the thrill of American football. Inspired by the golden age of retro gaming, this title puts you in the role of both head coach and star quarterback. Your mission is simple yet challenging: build a winning team, lead them through intense matches, and claim the championship trophy.

What makes Retro Bowl stand out is its unique mix of on-field action and off-field management. You’re not just running plays — you’re also making key decisions about player trades, training schedules, and team morale. Each choice affects your journey, and every match is a new chance to prove your skills.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to move your quarterback and dodge incoming defenders. Aim and pass the ball using your mouse or designated pass key, and time your throws carefully to connect with your receivers. When running plays, focus on creating openings and avoiding tackles.

On mobile, simply tap and swipe to move, aim, and throw. The intuitive controls make it easy to pick up but hard to master — the better your timing, the higher your chances of scoring.
