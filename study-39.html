About this study

1v1.LOL – Build, Shoot, and Outplay

1v1.LOL is an online third-person shooter and building game inspired by battle royale mechanics. The main mode pits you against another player in a fast-paced duel where quick reflexes and smart building decide the winner. You can construct walls, ramps, and platforms to protect yourself or gain a height advantage while aiming to eliminate your opponent.

The game also offers training modes to improve your shooting accuracy and building speed, making it perfect for both casual play and competitive practice.

How to Play
On PC, use WASD to move, the mouse to aim and shoot, and number keys or Q/E to select building pieces. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick for movement and buttons for shooting and building. Combine precise shooting with fast construction to outsmart your opponent and secure the win!
