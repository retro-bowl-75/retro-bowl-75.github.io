8 Ball Pool – Classic Billiards Challenge

8 Ball Pool is a competitive online billiards game where you can test your skills against players from around the world. Played with the classic rules, one player takes solids and the other stripes, aiming to pocket all their assigned balls before sinking the 8 ball to win.

The game offers 1v1 matches, tournaments, and friendly games with friends. Winning earns you coins, which you can use to enter higher-stakes matches or upgrade your cues for better performance.

How to Play
On PC, use your mouse to aim your cue, drag back to set power, and release to shoot. On mobile, drag your finger to line up the shot, adjust the power, and lift to strike. Plan your shots carefully, control the cue ball’s position, and avoid sinking the 8 ball too early — or you’ll lose instantly!
