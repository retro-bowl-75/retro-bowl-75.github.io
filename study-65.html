Death Run 3D – Reflex Speed Challenge

Death Run 3D is a high-speed first-person runner that tests your reflexes to the extreme. You race through a neon tunnel filled with moving obstacles, sharp turns, and sudden gaps. The track moves at a relentless pace, leaving you only fractions of a second to react.

The game’s fast movement and unpredictable patterns make it a real challenge, perfect for players who enjoy pushing their reaction time to the limit. The further you go, the higher your score — but one mistake means starting over.

How to Play
On PC, use the arrow keys to move in four directions. On mobile, swipe in the desired direction to avoid obstacles. Stay focused, anticipate changes, and react instantly to survive. The key is to stay calm under pressure and keep your movements smooth. How long can you last in this intense reflex test?
